❌ Why OpenAlex Makes Critical Errors
  • 1. Academic Lexical Noise: OpenAlex matches words broadly across millions of non-chemistry papers. When querying CTAC CTAB mixed surfactant gold bipyramids 8-hydroxyquinoline kinetics, OpenAlex literally returned government administrative paperwork compliance text ("The public reporting burden for this collection...") and generic porous ceramics instead of anisotropic nanocrystals.
  • 2. Ignores Buried Supplementary Information (SI): Over 85% of physical chemistry recipes, molar ratios, and negative control runs are buried inside DOCX/PDF Supplementary Information files, which OpenAlex completely ignores.
  • 3. The "Textbook Majority Bias" Trap: OpenAlex ranks older, highly cited papers from 2005–2015. Over 90% of those papers study CTAB nanorods (where bromide = elongation). This floods the LLM context with the exact outdated heuristic that causes inverted predictions!
  • 4. Zero Contradiction Detection: OpenAlex only indexes metadata and citation counts; it cannot determine when two papers report opposing kinetic regimes or conflicting facet passivation mechanisms.
